SIH 2026 – Problem Statement SIH26134

ROLE
You are a senior product architect, UX designer, full-stack engineer and AI engineer.

Build a production-quality prototype called "SkillBridge AI".

The goal is to demonstrate an end-to-end skill-gap intelligence platform that connects:

IMPORTANT:

==================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

SkillBridge AI should answer:

"What skills does the student have?"
"What skills does industry require?"
"What is the actual gap?"
"What should the student learn next?"
"How job-ready is the student?"
"Which jobs match the student's current skills?"
"What skills are institutions lacking across their students?"

The system must combine evidence from:

1. Resume
2. Skill survey
3. Adaptive assessment
4. Target job requirements
5. Industry demand data

Do NOT treat the resume alone as the skill-gap source.
