---
name: Government-first product direction
description: Product sequencing for the Skill Bridge workforce intelligence platform.
---

Skill Bridge should be developed as a government workforce intelligence portal first. The student experience is a later phase and should remain clearly secondary until the government workflows are complete.

**Why:** The primary buyer and decision-maker is government: departments need district demand signals, curriculum interventions, and readiness-program evidence before student-facing pathways are expanded.

**How to apply:** Keep the default landing view, navigation, language, and new capabilities focused on department decisions. Place student surveys, resume matching, and career guidance under a clearly marked Phase 2 student area.
